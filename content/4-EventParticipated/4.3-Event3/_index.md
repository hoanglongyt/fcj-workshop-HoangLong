### Key Topic Highlights

#### 1. Conquering Agentic AI Build Week (FCAJ Organizers)
- **Core Motto**: *"Show Up. Build. Pitch. WIN!"* – Any builder of any age or background with an idea and determination can build, pitch, and succeed.
- **Key Achievement**: FCAJ teams stood out among over 300 global competitors to secure victory in the ASEAN regional Agentic AI Build Week.

#### 2. Solution Architect Professional Native App Architecture (Plan V Team)
- **AWS Native Application Design**: Applying high-level AWS Well-Architected Framework principles to Native Application models.
- **Agentic AI Integration**: Embedding intelligent AI Agents into navigation and business logic workflows to improve user experience and optimize operational costs.

#### 3. 24 Hours of Hackathon: Building, Failing & Learning (3KA Team)
- **Inspirational Message**: *"The best lessons never came from winning. They came from the messy, exhausting process"*.
- **24-Hour Experience**: Valuable lessons on time pressure management, team coordination during endless bug fixes, and refining product pitches for judges.

#### 4. Automating Financial Compliance Workflows with Agentic AI (Six Pillars Team)
- **Adaptive AML Workflow Engine**: Addressing anti-money laundering (AML) investigative data enrichment challenges.
- **Legal Compliance Automation**: Transforming hours of manual data searches into legally compliant, automated reports using Agentic AI capabilities.

### Event Objectives

- Celebrate and learn from the hands-on journey of top **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** teams outshining over 300 global builders at the **Agentic AI Build Week** competition.
- Explore advanced software architecture solutions integrating **Agentic AI** and **Native Applications** designed following AWS Solution Architect standards.
- Gain practical insights from 24 hours of continuous Hackathon challenges – from ideation and debugging (bug fixes) to product pitching.
- Discover automated business workflow solutions powered by AI across crowd monitoring (S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D), corporate strategic signal detection (Signal Scout), and anti-money laundering investigations (Adaptive AML Engine).

### Teams List & Detailed Project Presentations

#### 1. FCAJ Organizers
- **Topic:** *"FCAJ - Agentic AI Build Week: Show Up. Build. Pitch. WIN!"*
- **Content:** Celebrating the journey of the FCAJ community outperforming over 300 global teams to claim victory in the ASEAN regional Agentic AI buildathon.
- **Core Motto:** Any builder of any age or background with an idea and determination can build, pitch, and succeed.

#### 2. Plan V Team
- **Members:** Pham Tien Thuan Phat, Huynh Hoang Long, Le Minh Nghia, Tran Dai Vi, Nguyen An.
- **Project Name:** **Solution Architect Professional AI Native App**
- **Overview & Solution:**
  - Solves tight deadline pressures for Solution Architects designing AI/Cloud systems from customer SOP documents.
  - Analyzes natural language requirements and drafts high-level architecture options (supporting Hybrid-Cloud models).
  - Automatically generates editable Draw.io diagrams using official **AWS Architecture Icons**.
  - Produces directional AWS cost estimates for Singapore region (`ap-southeast-1`) and generates IaC (Infrastructure as Code).

#### 3. 3KA Team
- **Members:** Huynh An Khuong, Nguyen Quoc Huy, Ngo Quang Khoi, Hoang Le Thanh Duc, Dang Nguyen Phuoc Loc, Dang Truong Hung.
- **Project Name:** **S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D** (*Smart Human-flow Evaluation, Prediction, Hazard Detection, Response, and Dispatch*)
- **Topic:** *"Hackathon Journey: 24 Hours of Building, Failing, and Learning"*
- **Overview & Solution:**
  - Transforms standard camera footage into intelligent operational information: monitors crowd density, measures queue conditions, detects congestion risks, and dispatches staff in real-time.
  - **Tech Stack:** Computer Vision (YOLO + ByteTrack), Amazon SageMaker, Amazon Bedrock, AgentCore + Strands Agent, and React Monitoring Dashboard.
  - **24-Hour Lessons:** Overcoming initial AWS/AI learning curves, managing video latency, and debugging until 3 AM with the mindset *"Showing up is already half the battle"* and *"Small, finished work beats big, broken ideas"*.

#### 4. Signal Scout (Dream AI Team)
- **Members:** Le Tan Luc, Do Hoang Hieu, Trieu Quoc Hao, Nguyen Van Duy Khiem, Nguyen Cong Minh, Nguyen Tran Minh Quan.
- **Project Name:** **Signal Scout** (*Detect Corporate Strategic Changes Early*)
- **Overview & Solution:**
  - Detects early corporate strategic changes and restructuring signals to empower Risk Management & Enterprise Strategy teams.
  - Automated evidence collection and validation using AI (Apify, TinyFish, LangFuse), metric analysis, and scenario visualization via an Executive Dashboard.
  - **AWS Infrastructure & Cost:** Leverages Amazon Bedrock, AgentCore Runtime, AgentCore Short-Term Memory, AWS WAF, AWS Amplify Hosting, DynamoDB, Lambda, Route53 with cost efficiency ($17 - $130/month).

#### 5. Six Pillars Team
- **Members:** Bui Hoang Viet, Nguyen Lam Anh, Nguyen Van Linh, Nguyen Canh Nguyen, Nguyen Minh Nhat, Tran Phuong Huyen.
- **Project Name:** **Adaptive AML Workflow Engine**
- **Overview & Solution:**
  - Automates investigative data enrichment for Anti-Money Laundering (AML) compliance workflows.
  - Transforms hours of manual searching into legally compliant, automated reports using Agentic AI capabilities.
